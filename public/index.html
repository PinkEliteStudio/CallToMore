From Broken to Blessed

After my divorce, my hope, joy, and smile were taken from me. But when I got serious with God, everything changed.

I learned there are no shortcuts to success. We must be willing to do the work and grind with faith.

The foundation of Call To More is rooted in Genesis 16 — the story of Hagar. Just as God saw Hagar in the wilderness and called her by name, He sees you too. He is "El Roi" — the God who sees me.

Now I walk with purpose as your cheerleader of hope, encouraging you to seek God first and step into the "more" He has for you.
