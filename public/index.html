From Broken to Blessed

After my divorce, my hope, my smile, my life, and even my joy were stolen. I felt lost. But when I got serious with God — no shortcuts — everything changed.

I discovered the power of seeking Him first. I learned that we are already blessed from the beginning (Genesis 1:29), and God's will has the blueprint for our future. We just need faith and intention to grab it.

God is the God of restoration. He promises to repay what the locust has eaten (Joel 2:25) and that the thief must restore sevenfold (Proverbs 6:31).

There are no shortcuts to success. You have to be willing to do the work and grind in faith. I'm here as your cheerleader of hope to encourage you every step of the way.
