Why I Wrote This

I did not write this planner because of something I observed in other women. I wrote it because of what I lived.

I came out of a marriage marked by narcissism and infidelity — and a pattern that repeated because there was never any true repentance behind the apologies. When it ended, that season was lonely, frightening, and heavy in a way I could not explain to most people.

It took prayer. It took a real relationship with Jesus Christ. And it took the right mentors — there were very few of them. The foundation of Call To More is Genesis 16 — Hagar in the wilderness, seen by name. He is El Roi, the God who sees me.

Let me say clearly what this is not: I am not celebrating divorce. Good men exist. Good marriages exist. But our choices carry consequences — and who you marry matters most of all.

This Is Not a Template

Most faith planners hand you blank prayer pages and a gratitude list. This is 155 pages of original theology — Hebrew and Greek word studies, scripture in three translations, and ten sections written out of one woman's testimony.

Section One — Name It Without Shame

Grief, loss, betrayal. Psalm 34:18 has the name of Jesus buried inside its final verb. You'll see it.

Section Two — Guard the Gate

The battle of the mind. Why one lie sticks harder than ten compliments — and how to demolish it.

Section Three — Open Hands

Surrender means releasing your will and your timeline. Never your safety. Know the difference.

Section Four — Choose Your Room

Community without codependency. Not everyone who loves God is equipped to speak into your wound.

Section Five — Break the Chain

Freedom from needing a person to feel whole. A good gift can become a terrible god.

Section Six — The Unseen Hour

Building good habits, killing the ones that are killing you. Start with thirty seconds.

Section Seven — Build the Vision

The eight biblical reasons a prayer goes unanswered — and how to correct seven of them today.

Section Eight — Seeking First

"Seek" in Matthew 6:33 is a present active imperative. Keep seeking. Until when? Forever.

Section Nine — The Blessing Already Yours

God blessed them before they had done anything. And what they meant for evil, He wove for good.

Section Ten — The Fence Was Always Love

Boundaries, levels of access, and honor. God's first conversation with man was about a boundary.

Read Three Pages From Inside

FROM SECTION SIX — THE ORIGINAL GREEK

ταμεᯮον (tameion) — the "inner room" of Matthew 6:6. It is the storeroom — the innermost windowless chamber where a household locked away its grain and its treasure. Jesus did not say go find a quiet spot. He said go to the room where the valuables are kept.

FROM SECTION TEN — LEVELS OF ACCESS

You do not give level ten access to a person who is giving you level one. And when you discover they are at a one, your job is not to demand they climb to a ten. Your assignment is to lower your access to match the level they are actually giving. Not out of revenge. Out of wisdom.

FROM SECTION TEN — ON FORGIVENESS

God never says "I will forget." He says "I will not remember." Those are not the same sentence. Remembering is not unforgiveness — it is how you stop walking into the same fire twice. When the memory comes and it does not come with pain, that is not a sign you failed to forget. That is the sign of release.
