Why I Wrote This

I did not write this planner because of something I observed in other women. I wrote it because of what I lived.

I came out of a marriage marked by narcissism and infidelity — and a pattern that repeated because there was never any true repentance behind the apologies. When it ended, that season was lonely, frightening, and heavy in a way I could not explain to most people.

It took prayer. It took a real relationship with Jesus Christ. And it took the right mentors — there were very few of them. The foundation of Call To More is Genesis 16 — Hagar in the wilderness, seen by name. He is El Roi, the God who sees me.

Let me say clearly what this is not: I am not celebrating divorce. Good men exist. Good marriages exist. But our choices carry consequences — and who you marry matters most of all.
