Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

Call To More ("we," "us," or "our") is operated by Rhina Delgado. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit calltomoreandyou.com, sign up for our free resources, purchase our products, or interact with us on social media.

We take your trust seriously. We will never sell your personal information.

Information We Collect

We only collect what we need to serve you:

  • Email address — when you request our free prayer guide, subscribe to our newsletter, or fill out a form on our site or on social media platforms.
  • Name — if you choose to provide it.
  • Purchase information — when you buy a product, payment is processed by PayPal or Amazon. We never see or store your full card details.
  • Usage data — basic, anonymous information about how visitors use our site, such as pages viewed, collected through cookies and analytics tools.

How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver the free resources you requested.
  • To send you encouragement, devotional content, scripture, and occasional updates about our products.
  • To fulfill and support your purchases.
  • To improve our website and content.

We do not send unsolicited messages, and every email we send includes a one-click unsubscribe link.

Email Communications

Our email list is managed through Kit (formerly ConvertKit). When you subscribe, you are agreeing to receive emails from Call To More. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any email, and we will remove you promptly. You may also email us to be removed.

Cookies & Tracking

Our site uses cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors interact with our pages. We use the Pinterest Tag to measure the performance of our content on Pinterest. If you arrive from TikTok, Instagram, or another platform, that platform may also use its own tracking. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to run our business. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Kit (ConvertKit) — email delivery and list management
  • PayPal — payment processing for digital products
  • Amazon — book sales and fulfillment
  • Netlify — website hosting
  • ManyChat — social media message automation
  • Pinterest, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube — social platforms where we share content

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Request that we correct or delete your information.
  • Unsubscribe from our emails at any time.
  • Opt out of cookies through your browser.

To make any of these requests, simply email us and we will take care of it.

Children's Privacy

Our website and products are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Data Security

We use reputable service providers with industry-standard security to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to safeguard what you share with us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
